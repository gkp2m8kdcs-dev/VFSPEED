How it works

Three steps. No streak guilt, no notifications begging for attention.

Enter your baseline

Income, monthly expenses, savings, debt, and a quick read on how stable your income and spending are.

Place a bet

Pick an amount and a direction — save more, cut a category, or pay down debt. VFSPEED shows the predicted score.

Check in & resolve

Next month, log a check-in. The bet is settled against the new score with a one-line read on what moved.

Privacy isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

VFSPEED is built so your financial data never has to leave your phone.

Everything stays on your device.

VFSPEED does not collect, transmit, or sell your financial data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, and no remote storage.

  • No tracking. NSPrivacyTracking is false.
  • No third-party SDKs reading your records.
  • No server. Wipe the app and your data is gone.

The only permissions we ever ask for

  • Photos (read) — to attach receipts to records.
  • Photos (write) — to save report images back to your library.
  • Camera — to capture receipts and documents.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people actually ask before installing.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your profile, transactions, history, and commitments are stored locally as a single JSON file in Application Support. The app has no remote endpoint.

Do you use analytics or ads?

No analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. The privacy manifest declares no tracking and no data collection.

Why do you need Photos and Camera?

Only to attach a receipt image to a transaction, or to save a chart/report back to your library. You can use the entire app without ever granting either permission.

What happens if I delete the app?

All local data — profile, history, transactions, and commitments — is removed with the app. You can also use Settings → Reset to wipe everything while keeping the app installed.

Can I export my data?

The data lives in a readable JSON file on your device. A future release may add a one-tap export; for now, contact support if you need a copy.

How is the score calculated?

Five weighted factors: emergency runway (30%), savings rate (25%), debt burden (20%), income stability (15%), and spending volatility (10%). Lower is calmer.
