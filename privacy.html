Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the VFSPEED mobile application (the "App"), published as VFSPEED for iOS. It describes how the App handles information when you install and use it. By installing or using the App you agree to the terms set out below.

This policy does not cover any third-party services you may reach by following an external link from the App. Those services have their own policies.

2. Data we collect

The App does not transmit data to us or to any third party. The only data the App stores is data you enter yourself, kept locally on your device. Concretely, this may include:

  • Your financial profile: monthly income, monthly expenses, total savings, total debt, income stability, and spending volatility.
  • Transactions you log: amount, category, note, and date.
  • Monthly check-in history (up to 12 months): year, month, anxiety score, income, and expenses.
  • Commitments ("bets"): kind, amount, predicted vs. actual score, timestamps, and resolution.
  • Your currency preference (e.g. USD, EUR, CNY).
  • Receipt or document images you choose to attach to a transaction, which remain on your device.

None of the above is sent off the device by the App. You can verify this by using the App with the device in airplane mode.

3. Data we explicitly do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number, or contact list.
  • No location, advertising ID, or device fingerprint.
  • No usage analytics, crash reports, or A/B test telemetry.
  • No advertising or marketing identifiers.
  • No purchase history or payment information.

The App's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false, an empty NSPrivacyTrackingDomains array, and an empty NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes array.

4. Permissions we request

The App requests the following system permissions, and only when needed:

Photos — read access (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription)

Used only when you choose to attach a photo to a transaction or record. The App does not scan or upload your photo library. If you grant limited access, only the specific items you select are accessible.

Photos — write access (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription)

Used only when you tap "Save to Photos" on a report or chart image. The App writes only the image you explicitly choose to save.

Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription)

Used only when you tap the in-app camera control to capture a receipt or document. The App does not record video or audio.

You can decline any of these permissions and continue to use every other feature of the App. Permission status is checked at the point of use and is never collected or transmitted.

5. Where your data lives

All App data is stored on your device inside the App's sandboxed Application Support directory, in a single JSON file (store.json) managed by the App. Photos and documents you import live in your system Photos library under your control.

You may delete your data at any time:

  • In-app: Settings → Reset wipes the profile, history, transactions, and commitments.
  • By uninstalling: Removing the App from your device deletes its sandboxed storage.
  • iCloud / device backups: Standard iOS backup behaviour applies. You can exclude this App's data from backups via your device Settings.

6. Third parties

The App does not embed third-party analytics, advertising, social, payment, or crash-reporting SDKs. The App does not contact any third-party domain at runtime.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and over. It does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or under the age required by your local law). Because no data is collected at all, this principle is enforced by design.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy is materially updated, the change will be listed in the App's release notes and the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the App after an update indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
